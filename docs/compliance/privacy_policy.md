## Important Information for Parents

**EduLens is committed to protecting your child's privacy.**

This Privacy Policy explains how EduLens collects, uses, and protects information when your child uses our educational tutoring application. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and take your child's privacy very seriously.

**Key Privacy Protections:**
- We do NOT collect your child's name, email, or other personal information
- We do NOT store photos or voice recordings
- We do NOT share your child's information with third parties
- We do NOT allow advertising or tracking in our app
- We require YOUR permission before collecting any information

Please read this policy carefully and contact us with any questions at **privacy@edulens.com**.

---

## 1. What Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information About Your Child

**We collect VERY LIMITED information about your child:**

#### What We DO NOT Collect:
- ❌ Your child's name
- ❌ Your child's email address
- ❌ Your child's photograph (stored)
- ❌ Your child's voice recording (stored)
- ❌ Your child's physical address
- ❌ Your child's phone number
- ❌ Your child's social security number
- ❌ Your child's precise location
- ❌ Your child's biometric data
- ❌ Any persistent identifiers linked to your child

#### What We DO Collect (Temporarily):

**Camera Images (NOT STORED):**
- **What:** Photos of homework, textbooks, or learning materials
- **Why:** To recognize math problems, read questions, and provide tutoring help
- **How Long:** Processed on your device INSTANTLY and deleted immediately (within seconds)
- **Where:** Never leaves your device, never uploaded to the cloud, never stored
- **Your Rights:** Required for homework help feature, you control when camera is used

**Voice Commands (NOT STORED):**
- **What:** Your child's voice when asking questions or giving commands
- **Why:** To understand questions and provide voice-based tutoring
- **How Long:** Processed on your device INSTANTLY and deleted immediately (within seconds)
- **Where:** Never leaves your device, never uploaded to the cloud, never stored
- **Your Rights:** Required for voice tutoring feature, you control when microphone is used

**Session Information (TEMPORARY):**
- **What:** What subject your child is studying (math, science, etc.)
- **Why:** To provide continuous help during a homework session
- **How Long:** Kept in device memory only while app is active, deleted when app closes or device sleeps
- **Where:** Stored only on your device, never uploaded
- **Your Rights:** Necessary for tutoring to work properly

**Anonymous Usage Statistics (OPTIONAL):**
- **What:** Anonymous counts like "app opened 5 times today" or "math problems solved: 10"
- **Why:** To improve the app and fix problems
- **How Long:** Up to 90 days
- **Where:** Stored anonymously, cannot be linked to your child
- **Your Rights:** OPTIONAL - You can opt out anytime

### 1.2 Information About You (Parent/Guardian)

**We collect information from YOU to manage your account and verify parental consent:**

**Required Information:**
- Your email address (for account login and important notifications)

**Optional Information:**

**How We Use Your Information:**

- Provide customer support
- Protect against fraud and abuse

## 3. How We Protect Information

### 3.1 Technical Safeguards

**On-Device Processing:**
- Camera images and voice recordings are processed entirely on your device
- No images or audio are ever uploaded to the internet
- Sensitive data is stored in secure, encrypted device memory
- All sensitive data is automatically deleted within seconds

**Encryption:**
- Your account information is encrypted with bank-level security (AES-256)
- All communications use secure HTTPS connections
- Payment information is handled by PCI-compliant payment processors

**Automatic Deletion:**
- Camera images: Deleted INSTANTLY after processing (0 seconds retention)
- Voice recordings: Deleted INSTANTLY after processing (0 seconds retention)
- Session data: Deleted when app closes or device sleeps (max 1 hour)
- Anonymous statistics: Deleted after 90 days

### 3.2 Access Controls

- Only authorized personnel can access account data
- All access is logged and monitored
- Multi-factor authentication required for staff access
- Regular security audits and penetration testing

### 3.3 Physical Security

- Data centers with 24/7 monitoring and security
- Redundant backups for account data (not child data)
- Disaster recovery procedures
- Industry-standard certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001)

## 5. Your Parental Rights (COPPA Rights)

### 5.1 Right to Review

You have the right to:
- Review any personal information we have collected from your child
- Request a description of the information collected
- Request deletion of your child's information

**How to exercise:** Email privacy@edulens.com with "COPPA Review Request"

### 5.2 Right to Refuse or Revoke Consent

You have the right to:
- Refuse to allow further collection of your child's information
- Revoke your previous consent at any time
- Delete your child's account and all associated data

**How to exercise:**
- **In-app:** Settings > Privacy > Revoke Consent
- **Email:** privacy@edulens.com with "Revoke Consent"
- **Phone:** 1-800-EDU-LENS (1-800-338-5367)

**What happens when you revoke consent:**
- All data associated with your child is immediately deleted
- You will receive confirmation within 24 hours
- Your child can continue using the app with on-device-only features
- You can re-consent at any time

### 5.3 Right to Data Portability

You have the right to:
- Request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format
- Transfer your data to another service

**How to exercise:** Email privacy@edulens.com with "Data Portability Request"

### 5.4 Right to Deletion (Right to be Forgotten)

You have the right to:
- Request complete deletion of all data
- Receive confirmation of deletion

**How to exercise:**
- **In-app:** Settings > Account > Delete Account
- **Email:** privacy@edulens.com with "Account Deletion Request"

**Deletion timeline:**
- Child data: Deleted IMMEDIATELY (within seconds)
- Account data: Deleted within 30 days
- Backup deletion: Completed within 90 days
- Legal retention: Some records kept for 7 years for compliance (consent records, financial records)

Audio Processing Pipeline
==========================

Microphone (16kHz) 
    ↓
Audio Buffer (1.5s)
    ↓
Voice Activity Detection
    ↓
Noise Estimation & Reduction
    ↓
MFCC Extraction (13 + Δ + ΔΔ = 39 features)
    ↓
Feature Normalization
    ↓
Model Inference
    ↓
Threshold Decision
    ↓
Callback Notification
